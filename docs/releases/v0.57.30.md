# Elyndra v0.57.30

- Scene Narrator may use one short, natural line of dialogue only when the current action contains speech intent or grounded visible evidence shows an NPC response.
- Player dialogue may only quote or faithfully paraphrase what the player actually expressed; it may not invent commitments, positions, questions, or decisions.
- NPC dialogue is bounded by player-visible facts, current role/description/trust, and the Engine-confirmed outcome; hidden Canon and unknown names remain unavailable.
- Pure observation/travel turns remain dialogue-free unless there is grounded NPC response evidence.
- Validation remains non-download only.
